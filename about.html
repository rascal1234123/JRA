About John

Authentic. Bold. Timeless.

John has worked across major publishers and recognised properties, producing covers, sequential pages, character art and commissioned work.

His professional credits include work connected with Marvel, IDW, Zenescope, G.I. Joe and Action Man. This page provides a dedicated home for John’s full biography, career history, selected credits and professional information.
