About John

Authentic. Bold. Timeless.

John Royle is a British comic book artist known for energetic storytelling, powerful anatomy and expressive, instantly readable character work.

His professional credits include work connected with Marvel, IDW, Zenescope, G.I. Joe and Action Man. This first build provides a strong editorial framework ready for John’s final biography, selected credits and approved imagery.
